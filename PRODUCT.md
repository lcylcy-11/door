# Product

## Canonical version

main is authoritative. The active local game is /horror/. Prior /2d/ and focus-cut sources remain historical.

## Current contract — 2026-10-28

A monochrome SVG pixel/stipple horror game in a recurring institutional waiting corridor. Observe environmental signals and manipulate each door. No visible instruction prose, menus or status labels. Text is confined to collected note backs; lock digits are mechanical glyphs. Start directly, click the finished scene to retry, Esc pauses.

Ten rooms: left slide, right slide, key and padlock, visible patrol silhouette, clock-photo combination memo, real peephole inspection and footsteps, vertical hinge-pin extraction and swinging door, angle-and-torque lockpick, mandatory ghost fishing duel followed by closing and reopening, golf-club barricade destruction. Items and solved hardware survive checkpoint retry. Full restart clears them.

Opening while a patrol is present causes a recoverable, telegraphed tug. Keep the pointer-adjacent effort needle in the moving target range and close against the pull, then seal during ease. Silence and absence of the silhouette share the actual patrol clock. The mandatory ambush cannot be skipped by a fast opening.

Crossing uses a spatial portal camera, perspective growth, a projected aperture mask, head movement and footfalls. No two-room opacity crossfade. The next room reaches its final camera pose continuously.

## Story direction

The protagonist was an upstairs noise perpetrator. The trapped loop is revenge by the downstairs victim, who died by suicide following the conflict. This production premise is never spelled out in the game. No method or graphic depiction. Environment metaphors: matching ceiling/floor bootprints, downward cracks, folded complaint papers, an ear-covering figure, an empty chair and flower, two stopped/misaligned clocks. The ordinary-looking collected pixel photographs show a housewarming party with a floor speaker, late-night furniture movement, and indoor golf practice. Only their reverse sides carry short ambiguous fragments. The code memo uses three clock faces (3,1,7), not a written password.

## Art and constraints

All active visual assets are original code-native SVG; no raster image generation or image download is used by this version. Fine black/white stipple and layered pixel geometry let doors, silhouettes, hardware, objects and camera move independently. Existing audio WAVs are reused locally. Static local prototype; no deployment, accounts, analytics or external runtime libraries. Subjective fear, mouse feel and puzzle readability remain for human play review.
