# Product

## Canonical version

`main` is authoritative. The current local game is `/horror/`; `/2d/` remains the earlier concept.

## Latest user direction — 2026-10-28

Discard unfun gimmicks and keep the fun parts. The chosen edit prioritises direct control: slow door movement with optional sound cover, one force-resistance hand encounter, and closing while looking away from the eyes. This is an editorial judgment for user play review, not a claim that fun has been objectively verified.

## Focus cut

One short encounter: opening → light cuts out → one telegraphed hand fight → eyes during closing → one-click lock → ending. No mimic repetition, drawer visits, wedge management, switch chores, detent/rhythm puzzle, scripted forced reopening, or repeated final hand. Slow movement is always viable; outside sound creates an opportunity to go faster.

## Constraints

Static local prototype with no external runtime libraries, deployment, accounts, analytics or persistent saves. Keep audio captions and visual cues, accessibility alternatives, pause and encounter retry. Preserve the existing environment art and the H2 resistance constants. Human fear, pacing and mouse feel remain subject to play review.
